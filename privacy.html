Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

LH Social Scheduler ("the app") is a desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer. It publishes the owner's own videos and photos to the owner's own Facebook Pages, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts, and shows the owner the performance of those accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by each platform after the owner approves access.
  • Basic account information: account ID, name/username, avatar and, for TikTok, the posting options the account allows (who can view, comment, duet and stitch settings).
  • Statistics about the owner's own accounts and posts: follower counts, and views, likes, comments and shares of the owner's own recent posts. These are shown only to the owner inside the app.
  • Content the owner chooses to post: the video or photo, caption, location text and posting settings.

How it is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's posts at the times the owner chooses and to show the owner their own statistics. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Tokens, post files and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server of its own. It only sends data to the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google (YouTube), and sends the location text of a YouTube post to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to look up map coordinates.

Cookies and device storage

The app keeps its data in local files on the owner's computer, as described above. It does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking of any kind. This website does not set cookies or run analytics, and no third party serves content or advertising through the app or this website.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

LH Social Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • YouTube / Google: Google security settings → Third-party apps
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions

Deleting the app's tokens and analytics folders on the owner's computer removes all stored data. Stored statistics are refreshed at most daily and kept only on that computer. Step-by-step instructions are on the data deletion page.

Other people

The app does not collect data about anyone other than the owner's own accounts. It is not directed at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the app's GitHub issues page.
