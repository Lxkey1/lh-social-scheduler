Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

LH Social Scheduler ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and publishes content to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data the app uses

The app uses only the access tokens and basic profile information (such as account ID and display name) that each platform provides after the owner signs in. It uses them only to upload and publish the owner's own posts.

Storage

Tokens and post files are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google (YouTube).

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Third-party users

The app does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings. Deleting the app's token files removes all stored data.
