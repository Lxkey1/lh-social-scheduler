LH Social Scheduler

LH Social Scheduler is a private, self-hosted desktop tool that the owner uses to schedule and publish videos and photos to their own Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and is used only with accounts the owner controls.

What it does

  • Schedules posts. The owner picks a video or photo, writes a caption, sets the posting options and chooses when it should go out.
  • Publishes to the owner's own accounts. At the chosen time the app uploads the post through the official APIs of each platform:
    • Facebook Pages and Instagram, through the Meta Graph API
    • TikTok, through the TikTok Content Posting API, using the privacy, comment, duet and stitch options the account allows
    • YouTube, through the YouTube Data API
  • Shows the owner their own statistics. Follower counts and the views, likes, comments and shares of the owner's own recent posts, shown only inside the app.

How it handles data

The app runs on the owner's computer and has no server of its own. Sign-in tokens, post files and statistics are kept only on that computer, and nothing is sold or shared. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is used and how to revoke access.
